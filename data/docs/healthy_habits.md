# Healthy Habits

## Physical activity
Adults should aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, such as brisk walking, cycling or swimming, plus muscle-strengthening exercises on 2 or more days. Even short 10 minute walks add up. If you have a health condition or have been inactive for a long time, check with a doctor before starting hard exercise.

## Handwashing
Wash your hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds: before eating, after using the toilet, after coughing or sneezing, and when you come home from outside. Use an alcohol-based hand rub if soap and water are not available.

## Eye care for screen users
Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something about 20 feet (6 metres) away for 20 seconds. Blink often, keep the screen slightly below eye level, and reduce glare. See an eye doctor if you have eye pain, sudden vision changes or constant headaches.

## Posture while studying or working
Sit with your back supported and feet flat on the floor. Keep the screen at eye level and the elbows at about 90 degrees. Stand up and stretch every 30 to 60 minutes.

## Avoiding tobacco and limiting alcohol
Tobacco in any form harms the heart, lungs and mouth, and quitting at any age helps. If you want to quit, talk to a doctor about support. Avoid alcohol, or keep it minimal.
