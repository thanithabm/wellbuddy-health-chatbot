# First Aid Basics

## Minor cuts and scrapes
Wash your hands first. Press a clean cloth firmly on the cut to stop the bleeding. Rinse the wound with clean running water, then cover it with a clean sterile bandage. Change the bandage daily. See a doctor if the bleeding does not stop after 10 minutes of firm pressure, the cut is deep or very dirty, or you have not had a tetanus shot recently.

## Minor burns
Cool the burn under cool running water for 20 minutes. Do not use ice, butter, toothpaste or oil. Do not burst blisters. Cover loosely with a clean non-stick dressing. Get medical help for burns that are large, on the face, hands, feet or private areas, deep, or caused by chemicals or electricity.

## Nosebleed
Sit up and lean slightly forward. Pinch the soft part of the nose firmly for 10 minutes and breathe through the mouth. Do not tilt the head back. Get medical help if the bleeding lasts more than 20 minutes or follows a head injury.

## Sprains
For a sprained ankle or wrist, rest the joint, apply a cold pack wrapped in cloth for about 15 to 20 minutes at a time, gently compress with a bandage and keep the limb raised. See a doctor if you cannot put weight on it, it looks deformed, or the swelling and pain are severe.

## Fainting
If someone faints, lay them flat on their back and raise their legs slightly. Loosen tight clothing and make sure they get fresh air. They should recover within a minute or two. Call for emergency help if they do not wake up quickly, are injured, or have chest pain or trouble breathing.
