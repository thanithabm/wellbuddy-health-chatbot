# Nutrition and Hydration

## Balanced plate
A simple healthy plate is about half vegetables and fruits, about one quarter whole grains such as brown rice, millets or whole wheat, and about one quarter protein such as dal, beans, eggs, fish, paneer or chicken. Choose healthy fats like nuts and seeds, and limit deep-fried food, sweets and processed snacks.

## How much water to drink
Many adults need roughly 2 to 3 litres of fluids a day, including water, buttermilk, coconut water and other drinks. You need more in hot weather, during exercise or when you are ill. A simple check is pale yellow urine. Very dark urine can mean you need more fluids.

## Sugary drinks and snacks
Sugary drinks like soft drinks, packaged juices and energy drinks add a lot of sugar with little nutrition. Choose water, lemon water, buttermilk or whole fruit instead. Eating too much sugar over time raises the risk of weight gain, tooth decay and type 2 diabetes.

## Breakfast and regular meals
Eating regular meals helps keep energy and focus steady. Skipping meals can cause tiredness, headaches and overeating later. A good breakfast includes protein and fibre, for example idli with sambar, eggs with toast, or oats with fruit.

## Healthy snacks for students
Good snack choices are fruit, roasted chana, nuts, curd, sprouts or a boiled egg. Keep a water bottle with you while studying.
