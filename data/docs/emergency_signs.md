# When to Seek Emergency Help

## Call emergency services immediately
In India, call 112 (national emergency number) or go to the nearest hospital emergency department right away for any of these: chest pain or pressure, severe difficulty breathing, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding that will not stop, loss of consciousness, a seizure, a suspected poisoning or overdose, a severe allergic reaction with swelling of the face or throat, or a serious injury.

## Signs of a stroke
Remember FAST. Face drooping on one side, Arm weakness or numbness, Speech difficulty or slurred speech, and Time to call emergency services immediately. Do not wait to see if it gets better.

## Signs of a heart attack
Chest pain, pressure or tightness, pain spreading to the arm, jaw or back, shortness of breath, cold sweat, nausea or dizziness. Call emergency services immediately and keep the person calm and seated.

## Severe allergic reaction
Swelling of the lips, face or throat, difficulty breathing, wheezing, widespread hives or feeling faint after eating, an insect sting or taking a medicine needs emergency care immediately.

## This assistant is not for emergencies
This assistant gives general health information only. It cannot examine you, diagnose a condition or replace a doctor. If you think it might be an emergency, do not wait for a chatbot: call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.
