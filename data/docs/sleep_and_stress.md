# Sleep and Stress

## How much sleep you need
Most adults need 7 to 9 hours of sleep each night. Teenagers need about 8 to 10 hours. Regularly sleeping much less can cause poor concentration, low mood and a weaker immune system.

## Tips for better sleep
Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, even on weekends. Avoid screens for about an hour before bed. Avoid caffeine (tea, coffee, cola, energy drinks) in the late afternoon and evening. Keep the bedroom dark, quiet and cool. Do not eat a heavy meal right before sleep.

## Managing everyday stress
Short daily habits help with stress: take a 10 to 15 minute walk, do slow deep breathing, stretch, listen to calming music, and talk to a friend or family member. Break big tasks into small steps and take short breaks while studying.

## A simple breathing exercise
Sit comfortably. Breathe in slowly through your nose for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, breathe out slowly for 4 seconds, and hold for 4 seconds. Repeat for 2 to 5 minutes.

## When to get help for your mood
Feeling stressed, anxious or low sometimes is normal. Talk to a doctor, counsellor or a trusted person if low mood, constant worry or loss of interest in daily life lasts for more than 2 weeks, or if it affects study, work or relationships.
